## Brand & Style

This design system expresses high-velocity precision, cognitive authority, and executive clarity for growth operators, media buyers, and CMOs managing multi-channel advertising ecosystems. The product sits at the intersection of predictive AI automation, CRM closed-loop attribution, and proactive competitive intelligence.

The visual style merges **Glassmorphism** with an ultra-refined **Corporate Modern Tech** aesthetic:
- **Tone:** Analytical, razor-sharp, forward-looking, and decisive.
- **Atmosphere:** Deep-space dark canvas punctuated by calculated neon luminescence, evoking low-latency trading terminals and mission-control cockpits.
- **Copy & Localization:** Native Brazilian Portuguese tailored for B2B performance marketing and executive reporting (e.g., "Desempenho de Campanhas", "Retorno sobre Investimento Publicitário - ROAS", "Inteligência Concorrencial", "Insights Preditivos de IA").
- **Mobile-First Realism:** Prioritizes dense, high-readability metrics, gestural sheet controls, and edge-to-edge situational awareness on phone screens, scaling seamlessly to ultra-wide desktop dashboards.

## Colors

The palette leverages high-contrast luminescence over multi-tiered dark foundation surfaces.

### Foundation & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#0B0F19`):** Deep slate-indigo backdrop providing void contrast.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#111827`):** Base structural container for card substrates and navigation rails.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#1E293B`):** Elevated interactive components, dialogs, and segmented toggles.
- **Surface Level 3 / Glass Tint (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.7)`): Frosted panels with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`.
- **Border Subtle (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`):** Hairline edge delineation.
- **Border Focus / Hover (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.4)`):** Kinetic interaction cue.

### Accents & Data Roles
- **Primary Indigo (`#6366F1`):** Core action state, primary KPIs, interactive focus rings, active navigation items.
- **Secondary Electric Violet (`#8B5CF6`):** AI triggers, automated budget optimization highlights, neural recommendations.
- **Cyber Emerald (`#10B981`):** Positive delta, healthy campaign ROAS, scaled conversion volumes, live sync state.
- **Cyber Cyan (`#06B6D4`):** Competitor tracking indicators, impression volume, reach metrics, secondary analytics.
- **Amber Warning (`#F59E0B`):** Budget cap exhaustion risks, creative fatigue warnings.
- **Crimson Negative (`#EF4444`):** Escalated CPA, churn risk alerts, disconnected ad channels.

## Typography

Typography pairs the geometry and character of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for executive headlines and high-impact numerical readouts with the optical legibility of **Inter** for dense transactional tables, configuration forms, and UI chrome.

- **Tabular Figures:** All metrics, timestamps, and currency values must utilize monospace numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent layout shift during live automated re-balancing.
- **Mobile Constraints:** Display levels compress on viewports `< 640px` to maintain KPI prominence above the fold without truncating critical currency symbols (`R$`) or percentage notations.
- **Language Adaptations:** Portuguese syntax tends to be 15–25% longer than English equivalents; button text and badge labels use strict truncation rules with tooltips and fluid flex wrapping.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an adaptive 8-point vertical grid architecture with fluid horizontal constraints optimized for handheld thumb-zones, progressing into structured modular grids on wider viewports.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Grid
- **Mobile (360px – 639px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin: 1rem` (16px) and `gutter: 1rem` (16px). Single-column metric stacking, swipeable horizontal metric carousels, and persistent bottom utility navigation.
- **Tablet (640px – 1023px):** 8-column fluid grid, `margin: 1.5rem` (24px). Split KPI views with 2-column card structures.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid anchored to a max-width of 1440px with `margin: 2.5rem` (40px) and `gutter-desktop: 1.5rem` (24px). Collapsible persistent left rail (navigation and AI agent status) with dual-column analytics canvases.

### Rhythm
Content density remains compact without cognitive clutter. Data-dense cards apply `space-md` internal padding, while macro container margins maintain `space-xl` breathing room to sustain luxury tech elegance.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses three stacked strategies: luminous atmospheric layering, physical hairline borders, and translucent glass refraction.

1. **Surface Tiers (Base Depth):**
   - *Ground:* `#0B0F19` canvas.
   - *Panels & Cards:* `#111827` elevated against the canvas.
   - *Floating Sheets & Modals:* `#1E293B` raised above overlays.

2. **Hairline Precision Borders:**
   To retain clarity in dark mode without muddiness, every container uses a calibrated border:
   - Default: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
   - Active/Selected: `1px solid rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.5)`.

3. **Glow & Shadow Profile:**
   - Soft Ambient: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`.
   - AI Kinetic Glow: `0 0 24px -2px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.25)` applied to autonomous agent recommendation banners and high-yield alerts.
   - Frosted Glassmorphism: Surfaces holding transient state use `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over a background of `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.75)`.

## Shapes

The system standardizes on **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry:
- **Base Components (`rounded` / 8px):** Buttons, text inputs, metric chips, filter dropdowns.
- **Structural Substrates (`rounded-lg` / 16px):** Metric summary cards, CRM lead timeline panels, AI opportunity widgets.
- **Modals & Bottom Drawers (`rounded-xl` / 24px):** Mobile bottom drawers (top-left and top-right radii only), desktop notification hubs.
- **Badges & Status Micro-tags:** Fully rounded pills (`border-radius: 9999px`) to create clear separation between data tags and clickable actions.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Otimizar Orçamento", "Criar Campanha"):** Solid gradient fill from Indigo (`#6366F1`) to Electric Violet (`#8B5CF6`). Height: 44px (mobile touch target), padding: 0 20px, font: `label-md`, text: `#FFFFFF`. Pressed state scales down slightly (`0.98`) with heightened indigo glow.
- **Secondary Action (e.g., "Ver Detalhes"):** Transparent background, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `#E2E8F0`. Hover state elevates to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` fill.
- **AI Assistive Action:** Subdued violet surface `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.15)` with text `#A78BFA` and a leading spark icon.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Compact height (24px), horizontal padding (8px), font `label-sm`.
  - *Ativa (Active):* Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, text `#10B981`, leading 6px pulsing dot.
  - *Pausada (Paused):* Background `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.12)`, text `#94A3B8`.
  - *IA Otimizando (AI Tuning):* Background `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.15)`, text `#C4B5FD`.
- **Competitor Benchmark Badges:** Monospace numerical tags displaying rank deltas (e.g., `+14.2% ROAS vs Concorrente`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Height & Bounds:** 44px on mobile with an internal padding of 14px. Dark slate fill (`#111827`) with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Focus state transitions to `1px solid #6366F1` with an outer ring glow (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.2)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Rounded square (4px) or circle (18px diameter). Checked state fills `#6366F1` with crisp white SVG checkmarks.

### Cards & Metrics
- **Metric Cards (KPI Tiles):** `#111827` background, 16px internal padding, `rounded-lg` (16px), subtle perimeter outline. Contains micro-sparkline, label in `label-sm` (`#94A3B8`), metric value in `metric-stat-sm` or `metric-stat-lg`, and trend delta chip in the top right.
- **AI Recommendation Banner:** Semi-transparent surface featuring a linear gradient border and an electric-violet ambient glow. Displays an actionable prompt in Portuguese (e.g., *"Aumentar investimento na Campanha Meta Ads em 15% para maximizar ROAS previsto em 4.2x"*).
- **CRM Integration Feed Item:** Linear item layout with avatar/channel icon, contact identifier, lifecycle stage indicator, and attributable ad spend.
